How Capital Trace ranks records
Score

Records are scored by event type, directness, role significance, transaction size, watchlist relevance, source quality, and uncertainty penalties.

Evidence Grade

A-grade records are direct, recent, exact, and source-linked. Lower grades indicate delay, ambiguity, administrative noise, or weaker signal value.

Actionability

Actionability is a research label, not investment advice. It compresses the record into Research Now, Watch, Context Only, or Low Signal.

Lane diagnostics

Each refresh reports what forms were checked, the lookback window, records added, holdings parsed, and whether a lane found no records or failed.
